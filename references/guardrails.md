# Constraints, privacy and provenance

Separate project decisions from general production hygiene.

## Project-specific rules

Capture the owner's rules in the brief and decisions with exact scope. Do not inherit the NieZamulaj no-magic preference, gentle voice, warm palette, “progress stays” mechanics or medical-symbol exclusions for unrelated brands. A fantasy bookshop or health service needs its own brief.

For NieZamulaj, openly naming the intended audience was allowed; promising treatment effects was excluded. Do not turn “no medical claims” into erasing that audience. A unicorn picture was compatible with the project; it did not authorize magical rewards. These are case facts, not automatic rules for future projects.

Do not add unsupported efficacy claims, fabricated testimonials or implied product capabilities. If regulated wording matters, use current primary sources and identify the precise review needed; avoid routine blanket “ask a lawyer” gates.

## Public and private material

Inspect relevant repository rules and the owner's sharing intent. Use clearly fictional examples or neutral placeholders for public materials. Real business contact details or prices may be appropriate when intentionally authorized; private household, financial, medical or child details must not leak from source projects.

A made-up realistic task/reward can be useful for a local density study. Do not confuse it with actual owner data. Inspect generated assets and metadata as well as text. A gitignored folder prevents accidental tracking, not all sharing; check the final bundle's allowlist.

Do not change ignore rules or other repository configuration unless needed within the authorized scope. Do not publish the whole repository as a brand package.

## Rights and sources

Use original, licensed or authorized material. Avoid unauthorized imitation of identifiable characters or third-party marks in deliverables. For references, record what is inspiration and what may be redistributed.

For every shipped font and external asset record source, acquisition date, license/terms and permitted distribution. Preserve license files and verify the terms needed for embedding, logo use or redistribution. Paid licensed fonts can be valid; open fonts are a convenient choice, not a universal requirement.

For AI artwork record tool/model when exposed, exact prompt, source reference, date, revisions and unresolved defects. Do not invent commercial rights, trademark clearance, exclusivity or a font identity from generated pixels.

## Appropriate checks

Monochrome is essential for a specified home-print mode and useful for logo resilience. Do not force every colourful screen illustration into a duplicate B&W deliverable when the product has no such use. Demonstrate functional meaning without relying solely on colour where that matters.
