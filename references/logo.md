# Logo: preserve direction, finish the master

## Existing identity first

Inspect the actual supplied reference. Record its source path, dimensions and hash when useful. Determine whether the task is preserve, refine or redesign. Do not reopen a selected logo because a generic checklist asks for three concepts.

If only a small bitmap exists, use it unchanged for studies. Do not guess its font, swap in the headline font, independently crop an icon or wrap it in an SVG and call that a vector master. A faithful reconstruction is design work: compare silhouettes, slant, proportion, spacing, baseline, colour split and wordmark against the reference at enlarged and intended sizes.

If the owner wants exact preservation and you cannot reproduce it faithfully, obtain the source or ask for the specific permission needed to redraw/change it. Continue other work. A missing faithful master is an explicit final-logo blocker.

## New identity

When naming and lead direction support it, prepare a few distinct concepts derived from the brand's own story and objects. Show name spelling and case exactly. Compare in relevant placements rather than relying on huge centered marks. Use image generation for raster ideas if helpful; use vector tools/code for the final precise geometry.

After selection, prepare relevant variants:
- Primary full-colour lockup.
- One-colour dark and reversed versions.
- A compact lockup or separate mark only if useful and accepted.
- Transparent PNG exports and appropriate favicon/app-icon sizes when in scope.

Do not generate unused stamp, app or packaging variants by default.

## Production checks

Create clean, self-contained SVGs with correct viewBox, no unintended raster embedding or external fonts; outline wordmark paths when the licence permits. Retain an editable source and the exact font/license record if lettering stays live.

Inspect vectors and exported PNGs at actual small sizes, on light/dark backgrounds and in intended print. Check alpha edges, export padding and source fidelity. Report computed ppi for raster uses, but do not equate ppi with readability. Screen logos have different contrast exceptions from functional UI; do not claim the whole product accessible from logo tests.

Write `logo/usage.md`: variants, clear space tied to a visible unit, measured/tested minimum size, background rules and misuse examples. Distinguish a proposed minimum from an approved standard.

Check obvious similarities through current search if needed; this is a limited resemblance check, not legal clearance. Preserve provenance. Never silently ship an approximate replacement for a chosen reference.
