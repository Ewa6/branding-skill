# Naming when the name is open

Skip ideation if the owner has chosen a name. Offer proportionate screening if it is missing; do not delay unrelated branding while waiting on a blocked registry.

Write the naming brief from the product, audience, languages, desired feeling, commercial scope and owner's hard requirements. Do not assume one word, an available .com, no medical associations or bilingual pronunciation unless relevant.

## Bounded exploration

Use distinct registers (descriptive, emotional, unexpected) and produce a small shortlist. A small maker/checker team can help; see [team.md](team.md). Screen promising options before emotional investment, not every discarded sketch. A second pass should address a specific gap or new evidence, rather than simply produce more names.

Say each finalist aloud in context, test spelling and unwanted associations in intended languages, and explain its strongest risk. The owner can propose the winner; it need not come from the AI list.

## Evidence-based checks

Use current primary sources for official status. Record exact name/variant, jurisdiction or domain, source URL, date, result and limitation.

- Prior use: exact and similar spellings, relevant category, local-language search and actual business/app-store pages. A search with no hits establishes only “no conflict found in these searches”.
- Domains: discover the relevant registry via [IANA RDAP bootstrap](https://data.iana.org/rdap/dns.json) or current registry documentation. Distinguish registered record, no record returned, reserved/premium status and actual registrar availability. A timeout, blocking or rate limit is unverified. A 404 must be from a working authoritative domain endpoint, not a broken URL. Repeating the same lookup twice does not prove a name purchasable.
- Trademarks: use official offices for the intended markets, such as [EUIPO](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/), [TMview](https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/), [Polish Patent Office](https://uprp.gov.pl/) or [USPTO](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks). Search relevant classes and similar marks where tools permit. Third-party aggregators may suggest leads but do not establish legal clearance.
- Meaning: identify confusing, offensive or misleading associations with evidence. Health associations are evaluated against this brand's brief, not an automatic universal exclusion.

Use independent checking for the shortlist when feasible. Recheck a changed spelling or flawed method; avoid redundant requests merely to satisfy a quota. If an official source is unavailable, record the exact remaining check and continue useful work.

## Output

Write `brand/name/report.md`: recommendation and fallback, shortlist rationale, evidence table, checked-but-rejected alternatives and remaining uncertainties. Avoid a blanket FREE/CLEAN label; separate domain, prior-use and trademark findings.

The owner selects the name or delegates that choice. Registration, purchases and external filings are separate authorized actions. Screening is not a guarantee of exclusive rights.
