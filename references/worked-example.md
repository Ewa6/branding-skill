## 1. Brief, naming and voice

The product developed as a paper-first sticker game for solo adults and households, welcoming people who find starting tasks difficult. Polish leads; English treatment remained open. Earlier naming work used distinct makers and an independent checker, with blocked checks reported. The owner ultimately selected NieZamulaj herself.

Keep independent evidence-based screening; do not repeat weak “clean/free” claims or excessive redundant searches from the naming work. A reserved domain does not establish trademark rights.

The voice became warm, light and concrete: the name carries cheek, surrounding copy lowers pressure. Conditions could be named openly while treatment promises remained excluded. This distinction must survive summaries. No-magic and no-guilt rules were project choices, not universal brand principles.

The owner preferred “plansza” over “tor” and rejected AI-like punctuation in the slogan. The wording and line breaks could remain while punctuation changed. Local copy feedback did not require a new identity.

## 2. Make the direction desirable without losing function

A plain document-like result did not meet the owner's ambition. The Studio prototype strengthened typography, paper layering, stickers and editorial composition while preserving usable label spaces and separate screen layouts.

The owner liked that direction but rejected a starburst sticker symbol; it changed to a check. The three-strip brand mark was a different component and remained. Decorative background motion was explicitly deferred to programming.

Lesson: preserve the appealing aspects of the existing work and act on the precise criticism. “More personality” is not permission to alter physical requirements or implement future behavior.

## 3. Choose ingredients, not whole moodboards

For the later boards, the owner selected typography from board 02 and reduced geometric pictures, logo direction and quieter spacing from board 01. This overrode the AI recommendation to lead with a different board.

Sticker and picture family directions were subsequently approved. Their sheets were raster visual references, not transparent production assets, exact font specifications or final physical geometry.

Lesson: keep component-level choices and scope; develop selected elements individually.

## 4. Source fidelity and real typography

An arch/circle/bowl logo interpretation was superseded when the owner supplied the exact preferred reference: three tilted rounded terracotta strokes beside a continuous horizontal wordmark, Nie in dark ink and Zamulaj in terracotta.

The small supplied bitmap became the sole logo authority. Its exact lettering was unidentified. Substituting Manrope or the editorial serif would have changed it; no faithful production master was available at the end of this phase.

For headlines the team compared actual Bodoni Moda, Playfair Display and Cormorant Garamond files, including genuine italic, Polish glyphs and an optical cap-height approximation. The owner explicitly chose **B / Playfair Display 600**. That approved editorial type, not logo lettering or final practical-text type. Manrope remained a working baseline.

Lesson: preserve the reference, separate typography roles, and distinguish an actual font choice from generated appearance.

## 5. Print geometry and visual motivation

The owner clarified Fishero labels measure **30 × 14mm** and should fit comfortably. Old circular targets were superseded. The later working proposal used **34 × 18mm clear interior**, **35 × 19mm outer frame**; actual PDF interiors measured approximately 33.993 × 17.996mm. No physical print trial occurred.

Fit alone produced a dull specimen. The owner then identified missing image allocation, awkward arrows/lines, weak rewards, detached numbers and a noisy bottom rule. The revision added a fixed image area, attached numbers, pronounced reward tickets and a larger finale, with simple left-to-right groups. Arrows and the bottom rule were removed.

One later variation was labelled calm by the team but rejected as too chaotic by the owner. Other alternatives stayed exploratory. AI preference did not select them.

Lesson: measure usable space early, then assess personality, hierarchy and number association in the actual design. Do not revive superseded circles, connectors or the rejected route as current NieZamulaj instructions.

## 6. Motifs and populated-state critique

The owner asked for dinosaurs, cars and unicorns. An ambiguous “cards” was clarified as cars/vehicles. Picture swaps demonstrated compatibility with the palette but did not fully answer her question about board-level themes.

The subsequent comparison separated picture-only from optional restrained final-emblem accents. The base identity and label spaces remained fixed. Illustrations were somewhat more storybook-like than the approved geometric family, so compatibility was not approval of finished art.

Empty, 12/30 and full boards were then compared plain and themed, six examples in all. Actual renders revealed that repeated completion text and ticks made the full board administrative; a long reward had an orphan word; decorative dinosaur plates looked like a crown.

The revision used task-only labels, explicit short print titles while retaining originals, a balanced two-line reward and one optional subdued footprint. Eight component recipes captured the useful system. The art director, challenger and simulated viewer changed the work through concrete critique; the viewer was AI role-play, not research.

The owner said **“great, approved for now”** about that batch. It became the working direction. It did not approve every earlier B/C layout, all future art, exact print production or a final book.

## 7. Remaining preparation and honest handoff

A later authorized batch produced:
- 12 original SVG utility icons, checked at practical sizes.
- Six quiet/error-state recipes with capability-aware wording.
- Three companion printable layouts in colour/monochrome.
- 12 transparent picture candidates with prompts/provenance.
- Role-specific contrast and small-logo specimens.
- An A4 calibration PDF for a physical Fishero trial.

Review corrected an ambiguous utility icon, offline copy that assumed a paper board existed, monthly task/field pairing and confusing page names. Residual art consistency issues stayed explicit. These new studies remained proposals; round-11 approval did not extend to them.

The curated handoff excluded obsolete logo experiments from active authority and kept the exact supplied reference. Physical printing, faithful logo-master production, final artwork and final standards remained open. The intended 20–30 picture library was not complete merely because 12 candidates existed.

**NieZamulaj's final brand book and mockups were reserved for Claude Design. They were not completed in the inspected Codex phase.** The generic final-production workflow in this skill is newly specified for completing future projects inside Codex, not a claim that NieZamulaj already passed it.

## What transfers

Evidence-led intake; local feedback handling; component-level approval; exact source preservation; real font comparisons; geometry plus visual inspection; populated-state testing; distinct picture/theme/icon layers; useful bounded critique; and a curated current delivery with honest status.

The final book, vector production, shareable exports and packaging requirements in the skill extend these methods into a complete future workflow. They do not turn unresolved case details into approved brand standards.
