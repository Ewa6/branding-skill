# Graphic system: distinct jobs

Separate utility icons, decorative signature shapes, personal pictures and optional theme accents. Sharing colours does not make them interchangeable.

## Utility icons and signature components

Start with the actual actions and nouns the product needs. Create a small SVG family on one grid with consistent optical weight and corners; use precise SVG/code rather than forcing bitmap generation into vectors. Compare at intended 16/20/24px sizes and in context with labels.

Test semantics: can a paper-to-screen icon be mistaken for mail? Does a gift imply redemption mechanics the product does not have? Retain text labels for ambiguous actions. For state/control icons, inspect contrast and focus usage where applicable.

Signature shapes should have a purpose: completion badge, reward ticket, frame, divider. Document anatomy, padding, type and allowed variation in `components/`. Do not make every shape a separate logo.

## Picture library

Define the family before expanding: silhouette, detail, geometry, texture/shading, palette, optical weight, transparency and crop rules. Build a representative pilot of varied subjects, often four to six. An approved style does not automatically approve a generated batch.

Use the available image-generation capability for raster artwork, following its skill/tool instructions. Request a real transparent background when needed. Save original outputs and exact prompts. Do not crop a moodboard into supposed production assets or erase backgrounds with a different editing workflow contrary to tool rules.

Review each source at intended small/large allocation, on white and the product background, and in grayscale if the intended channel needs it. Inspect the alpha channel and visible edges; a checkerboard baked into pixels is not transparency. Look for pale shapes disappearing, extra limbs/marks, residual shading and inconsistent detail.

Expand to the number/subjects the product needs after the pilot. Twenty to thirty pictures may be a project target; it is not a prerequisite for every brand. Track each asset's status and rights in [the register](../assets/asset-register.csv). Do not mark an entire set approved because some examples were accepted.

## Themes beyond pictures

When requested, show the same layout with picture-only and restrained board-level accent modes. Keep fixed identity, practical text and usable areas constant. Test the theme's recognition and visual load when content is full, not just empty.

Optional thematic echoes should remain subordinate and removable. A picture choice need not change the brand palette or imply new product mechanics. Record what is fixed, what is configurable and the available plain version.

## Deliverables

Individual SVG icons plus a meaning/size sheet; individual production images plus gallery, alt-text guidance, prompts and provenance; signature-component recipes. Keep unrefined candidates clearly labelled and outside the production asset set.
